I work at the intersection of development and visual design. I enjoy not only writing code, but also thinking about how a product looks, feels and explains itself.

Clear structure, expressive typography and the details of interaction matter to me. I like turning complex ideas into simple interfaces.

This website is a place for my work and notes. I collect projects here, share the process and describe the decisions behind the result.
